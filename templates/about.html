The Story

Builder first.
Scientist second.

Started in Accra, Ghana — Economics and Statistics at the University of Ghana, learning to find patterns in data before data science became a career path. That foundation led me to Turin, Italy for an MSc in Stochastics and Data Science, where I co-authored research published in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics.

From there: Amsterdam (NLP research at Neosound), Florence (leading NLP projects at Binoocle), the United States (remote, building ML quality systems), Canberra (energy forecasting then data science), and now Brisbane — building ML and GenAI systems at Domino's Pizza Enterprises.

I deliver end-to-end solutions — from data systems to production infrastructure to deployed applications — rather than analyses that sit in reports.

What stayed consistent through those moves was not the job title. It was the habit of learning the domain quickly, building with rigor, and caring about whether the outcome works for the people depending on it.
